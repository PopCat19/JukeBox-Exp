About

JukeBox-Exp is an unofficial conceptual fork of JukeBox, originally developed by CoolTasDude, is a mod of Slarmoo's Box which is a mod of UltraBox which ported features from Pandora's Box into GoldBox which are both mods of JummBox which is a mod of BeepBox.

JukeBox-Exp does not claim ownership over songs created with it, so original songs belong to their authors.

Neither JukeBox-Exp nor its creators assume responsibility for any copyrighted material played on JukeBox-Exp. No songs are ever received, recorded, or distributed by JukeBox-Exp's servers. All song data is contained in the URL after the hash (#) mark, and JukeBox-Exp running inside your browser converts that data into sound waves.

JukeBox-Exp does not collect, track, or share any user data. Its full source is available in the GitHub repository.
